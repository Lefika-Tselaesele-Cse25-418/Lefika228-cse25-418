Company Story

About FIKSPOINT DEALERSHIP

A modern dealership platform designed to advertise, sell, and maintain quality vehicles for customers everywhere.

Objective

Our website combines vehicle advertisement, online purchase support, test-drive requests, and service bookings in one responsive user experience.

Target Audience

  • Drivers aged 18 and above.
  • Middle and upper middle-income customers.
  • People seeking new or pre-owned cars and service upgrades.

Website Goals

Wider Reach

Serve customers beyond physical dealership location through 24/7 online browsing.

Instant Inquiries

Support fast contact for vehicle prices, model availability, and test-drive scheduling.

Convenient Purchasing

Allow customers to shortlist and submit purchase requests directly online.

After-Sales Care

Enable returning customers to book routine service and upgrade appointments.

Technical Stack

  • HTML5 for structure.
  • CSS3 and custom theme for visual design.
  • Bootstrap 5 for responsive layout and components.
  • JavaScript for dynamic listings, forms, and checkout interactions.

Quality and Publishing

  • Cross-browser checks for Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
  • Navigation and mobile menu validation.
  • Structured for publishing to GitHub Pages.
  • Clear page taxonomy: Home, Listings, Details, Booking, About, Contact.

Future Development Opportunities

  • Add richer car attributes such as fuel consumption and drivetrain options.
  • Introduce secure full online payment processing.
  • Enable customer profile accounts and personalized dashboards.
  • Launch dedicated mobile app experience.
  • Integrate live chat for real-time support.
